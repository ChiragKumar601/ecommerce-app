Eligible items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Innerwear, lingerie, beauty and personal care, and baby care items are not returnable; each product page shows whether it can be returned. Refunds go back to the original payment method — card or UPI first, then gift card, then credits. Cash on delivery refunds are paid as credits. This is placeholder content for the demo store.
