This is a demonstration store. Please don't enter real personal or payment information.

**What we store.** If you create an account, we store the details you give us — such as your name, email or phone number, addresses and order history — so the store can work for you.

**Automatic deletion after 30 days of inactivity.** Accounts with no login or activity for 30 days are permanently deleted, together with their personal data (profile, addresses, saved cards, wallet, bag, wishlist, recent searches, support requests and orders). Reviews you wrote stay on product pages, shown as "Former customer".

**Requesting deletion.** You can ask us to delete your account at any time: go to Contact Us and raise a request of type "Account deletion". Your account and personal data will be deleted within 30 days — in practice, at the next daily clean-up.

**Card details.** We never store full card numbers or CVVs. Only test card numbers are accepted on this demo.
